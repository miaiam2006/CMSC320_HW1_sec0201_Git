Q2: What field could data science be applied to? Give an Example.

Answer: Agriculture! You could analyze how climate change is affecting crops and what to do about it.

Q3: What is the difference between Merging and Rebasing?

Answer: Merging changes the files to match the merged branch, rebasing matches the changes you made on the rebased branch and does them to the main branch
